===== MIGRATION ROADMAP =====

PHASE 1 — NOW (Free Plan, Stay Put)
- Keep publishing content on WordPress.com free
- Build calculators and assets locally (what we're doing)
- Prepare everything so migration is smooth when ready

PHASE 2 — SOON (Move to Paid Hosting)
Option A: WordPress.com Business Plan (~$25/month)
  - Easiest transition — no migration needed
  - Unlocks plugins and custom code (embed calculators immediately)
  - Best choice if you want low hassle

Option B: Self-Hosted on SiteGround (~$3-8/month)
  - More control, cheaper long-term
  - Requires migrating your site (doable, not hard)
  - WARNING: SiteGround deleted your entire site last time you cancelled
    → ALWAYS set up UpdraftPlus → Google Drive BEFORE doing anything else
    → NEVER cancel without downloading a full backup first

PHASE 3 — WHEN READY (Self-Host on Your Own PC)
- Stack: Ubuntu Server + Docker (WordPress + MariaDB + Nginx) + Cloudflare
- You already have a spare PC that can do this
- Web server (ports 80/443) and Minecraft server (port 25565) do NOT conflict
- Cloudflare handles your domain DNS + free SSL + DDoS protection


===== BACKUP STRATEGY (DO THIS FIRST ON ANY NEW HOST) =====

1. UpdraftPlus plugin → backs up files + database → sends to Google Drive automatically
2. GitHub repo for all custom code (calculators, etc.)
3. Annual WordPress XML export as a third safety net


===== MONETIZATION PLAN (IN ORDER) =====

STEP 1 — START NOW: Affiliate Marketing
- You already write book reviews → sign up for Amazon Associates
  - Add affiliate links to every book you review
- Personal finance niche affiliate programs:
  - M1 Finance, Betterment, Wealthfront (investing platforms)
  - YNAB (budgeting app) — great fit for your audience
  - Credit Karma, NerdWallet have affiliate programs too
- How it works: you get a small % when readers click your link and buy/sign up
- No minimum traffic required — start immediately
- Disclosure required: add "This post contains affiliate links" to relevant posts

STEP 2 — ADD EARLY: Email Newsletter
- Platform: MailerLite (free up to 1,000 subscribers)
- This is currently your biggest missing piece
- What to do:
  - Sign up for MailerLite (free)
  - Create a simple signup form
  - Add it to your sidebar, footer, and end of posts
  - Send a newsletter when you publish new posts
- Why it matters: email list = audience YOU own, not dependent on Google or social

STEP 3 — WHEN TRAFFIC BUILDS: Display Ads
- Start with: Google AdSense (no minimum traffic)
  - Apply once your site is on a paid plan (free plan blocks ads)
  - Pays very little at low traffic but it's passive income
- Upgrade to: Mediavine (requires 50,000 sessions/month)
  - Much higher RPM (revenue per thousand visitors)
  - This is the goal — it's where personal finance blogs make real money
- Do NOT bother with display ads until you have consistent traffic

STEP 4 — LATER: Higher-Tier Monetization
- Memberships / paid content (Patreon or WordPress membership plugins)
- Direct brand sponsorships (companies pay you to write about their product)
- Sponsored posts / reviews
- These require a real audience first — focus on building traffic before pursuing these

How to use calculators once on a paid plan:
- Files are already on GitHub Pages — just need to embed via iframe in WordPress
- Each calculator gets its own Page (not Post)
- Create a "Financial Calculators" parent page as a hub
- Each calculator page needs ~300 words of SEO content around the tool
- Add "Calculators" to your nav menu via Appearance → Menus
- iFrames are blocked on WordPress.com FREE — works once you upgrade to paid

YOUTUBE
- You don't need a separate platform — just paste a YouTube URL into a WordPress post
  and it embeds automatically
- Ideas: "walk through" videos of your calculators, personal finance tips, book reviews
- YouTube drives traffic back to your blog and vice versa

PODCAST
- Host audio on Buzzsprout (free tier up to 2 hours/month) or Transistor (~$19/month)
- Both give you an embeddable player to paste into your WordPress pages
- Good for commuters — extends your reach beyond readers
- Topics: same as your blog posts, or interview other personal finance bloggers

SEO BASICS
- Write for your niche: mortgage tips, budgeting, investing, book reviews
- Use your calculators as "pillar content" — they attract consistent search traffic
- Internal links: link your posts to your calculator pages and vice versa
- Each post should target a specific search phrase (e.g., "how to recast a mortgage")

  MailerLite (official plugin)     Connect your MailerLite signup forms directly in WordPress

  Pretty Links                     Simpler alternative to ThirstyAffiliates for link shortening

--- PERFORMANCE & ANALYTICS ---

  Google Site Kit                  Connect Search Console + Analytics from the WP dashboard
  Autoptimize                      Minify CSS/JS/HTML — pairs well with a caching plugin
