## On AI vs. Authentic Content

**Short answer: your voice is your moat. Don't give it up.**

Google's EEAT algorithm (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) now explicitly rewards first-person, real-world experience. A post titled *"We refinanced our car through our credit union and here's exactly what we saved"* beats any AI-generated explainer because no AI can authentically write your story.

**What AI is actually good for:**
- Generating outline ideas and section headers
- Suggesting keywords to research
- Proofreading your draft for clarity
- Brainstorming angles on a topic you already know

**What AI is bad for:** writing your posts for you. It won't sound like you — you already know this from trying it. Your readers follow *you*.

**The realistic approach when time is tight:** post less, but keep every post yours. One authentic post a month beats four AI posts that don't connect. Consistency matters, but quality and voice matter more at your traffic level.

## Research Topics

Study these before creating in each area. Each one will make your content significantly better.

### SEO & Blog
- [ ] **EEAT deep dive** — search "Google EEAT guide 2024" — understand why personal stories now outrank generic explainers
- [ ] **Google Search Console** — you already have it on your site; learn to find which posts are getting impressions but not clicks (easy wins)
- [ ] **Keyword research basics** — Ubersuggest free tier or just Google autocomplete; find what people actually search near your topics
- [ ] **Internal linking strategy** — linking your calculators from related posts boosts both post SEO and calculator traffic
- [ ] **Affiliate disclosure rules (FTC)** — quick read, required by law; simple to add a one-liner to posts with affiliate links

### YouTube
- [ ] **YouTube SEO basics** — titles, thumbnails, and descriptions drive discovery differently than Google; search "YouTube SEO for beginners 2024"
- [ ] **Hook, content, CTA structure** — the first 30 seconds determine if viewers stay; learn the formula before recording anything
- [ ] **Shorts algorithm vs. long-form** — Shorts build brand awareness but don't convert to subscribers as well; understand the tradeoff before going all-in
- [ ] **Batch recording workflow** — film 3–4 videos in one session to beat the time problem; search "batch filming YouTube videos"

### Email / Newsletter
- [ ] **MailerLite welcome sequence** — set up a 3-email sequence for new subscribers: welcome, best content, what to expect; this runs automatically forever once built

## YouTube Length Quick Reference

| Video Type | Target Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Calculator walkthrough | 5–10 min | Show the tool, walk a real scenario, explain each output |
| Explainer / tutorial | 8–15 min | Enough depth to be useful; don't pad for length |
| Personal story / vlog | 6–12 min | Conversational pace; edit ruthlessly |
| Shorts | 30–45 sec | One tip, one stat, or one "did you know" — that's it |
